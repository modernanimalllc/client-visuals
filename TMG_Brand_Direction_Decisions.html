Where we are

You have two strong bodies of brand work. Modern Animal’s recommended direction emerged from our 2025 strategy — personas, customer journey, positioning, and competitive analysis. Your team’s new direction emerged from the May website prototype and the July marketing videos. Encouragingly, they agree on the central shift — marriage prep is becoming lifelong formation — and both independently arrived at the same idea, “become the couple other couples want to become.” What remains is a short list of choices about how the brand looks, sounds, and whom it leads with.

Why reconciling now matters

A full set of videos and a new site prototype were produced before creative and strategy were reconciled. The work is strong; it simply preceded alignment, which is the natural cost of the two developing separately. Aligning them now, and keeping them aligned, ensures the next piece is on-strategy from the outset.

The one decision that drives the rest

Almost every choice below flows from a single question: whom does the brand lead with?

Modern Animal leads with aspiration. The faithful couple who sees marriage as a lifelong vocation — aiming a little higher so the brand inspires, and can stand next to Ascension, Word on Fire, and Hallow.

Your new direction leads with approachability. Meeting couples where they are first — busy, blended, interfaith, already civilly married — with a “welcoming, approachable” front door.

Both are faithful. The difference is the entry stance, not the depth of faith. It matters because it sets three things at once:

1. Brand & differentiation — whether TMG owns the premium “faithful formation” space or competes on accessibility.
2. Marketing & growth — who we target and how we spend: the aspirational funnel inspires upward and can capture the full market; the accessible funnel converts today’s couples faster.
3. Product design — how we build the experience: personalization, the first year of Marriage Every Day, and the fellowship features all calibrate to who we’re serving.

★ Our recommendation

Blend, with a clear hierarchy. Lead with the higher aspiration as the brand’s north star, and use approachability as the on-ramp. Keep the ceiling high; keep the door wide. This preserves the premium positioning our strategy was built to win while keeping the accessible, real-life welcome your videos and prototype do well — so none of the produced work is wasted.

Decisions — how the brand sounds

Where the two agree, there’s nothing to decide: positioning (prep → lifelong formation), the aspirational identity (“become the couple…”), and two audiences, two paths (couples vs. ministry leaders) are all aligned. The rows below are the real choices.

1. Who we lead with★ We suggest: Blend
Modern AnimalFaithful aspiration first — “welcoming, faithful.”
New directionApproachability first — “welcoming, approachable,” meet-you-where-you-are.
2. The emotional entry point★ We suggest: Blend
Modern AnimalOpen with invitation & joy — draw couples toward something meaningful.
New directionOpen by naming the risk — what’s at stake if formation stops at the wedding.

Directional only. We’re choosing a lean, not locking headlines — the example lines are illustrative, and we’ll experiment and lock final copy as we go. e.g. invitation: “Most marriage prep ends at the altar. Yours doesn’t have to.” · e.g. risk: “Support disappears after the wedding.”

3. The messaging pillars★ We suggest: Blend
Our four pillarsJoy · Shared Transformation · Beauty · Pilgrimage.
Their pillars (inferred)Formation-not-completion · Becoming · Personalization · Faithful & accessible · Community.

Their videos aren’t built on named pillars — we inferred the themes above from the talking points and prototype. Blend = keep our four pillars as the spine, strengthen the two they under-use (Joy, Beauty), and fold in their two additive themes — Personalization (row 6) and Community (fellowship features, pending the architecture freeze).

4. The word “flexible”★ We suggest: Blend
Modern AnimalSecondary reassurance (we retired the old “Faithful · Flexible · Fun” as the identity).
New directionBack as a headline theme (“faithful, flexible Pre-Cana formation”).
5. How present and consistent the faith is★ We suggest: Blend
Modern AnimalFaith as a consistent, invitational throughline — confident, woven through beauty and meaning (“timeless Catholic wisdom”), present without leading with doctrine.
New directionFaith shows up unevenly — strong in a few lines (marriages that “witness to the beauty of God’s design and inspire others to embrace the vocation of marriage”), but most top-of-funnel and couples copy stays faith-light and relational (“prepare for real life,” “faithful, joyful, strong”).

Distinct from row 1: row 1 is who we lead with; this is how present the faith is, and how consistently. Blend = carry faith as a steady invitational throughline, and make their strongest faith line an anchor — rather than letting the front of the funnel go faith-light.

6. Personalization — “meet each couple’s real story”★ We suggest: Blend
Modern AnimalPersonalization is present, but the lead is the journey — careful not to revert to convenience or logistics.
New directionPersonalization is the headline differentiator — built around your situation (blended, military, interfaith, convalidation, cohabiting).

Biggest lever on product design — how adaptive the experience actually is. Blend = elevate personalization to a lead promise, framed as meeting each couple’s real story, without letting convenience become the identity.

7. What proof leads★ We suggest: Blend
Modern AnimalChurch trust leads — “Promoted by the USCCB, accepted in parishes and dioceses worldwide.”
New directionScale & reviews lead — 100,000+ couples, 100+ countries, 4.5 on Google.

Blend = lead with church approval (your real differentiator), support with scale and reviews. Including the approval line at all is also flagged as a fix below.

Language note. Use “flexible & convenient,” not “self-paced.” “Self-paced” implies a solitary, do-it-yourself course, which works against TMG’s promise of accompaniment; “flexible & convenient” keeps the practical benefit without the go-it-alone connotation.

Decisions — how the brand looks

Aligned already (no decision needed): logo & Rose Icon, the cream/warm base, serif-for-emotion, and the rose watermark — your prototype moved toward all of these. The rows below are where it diverges.

Color depth (deep blue / gold): a planned future evolution. The deeper palette of deep blues and subtle gold was always scoped for a later phase, not this refresh, so the prototype’s charcoal, red, and cream is correct for now. Noted here so it stays on the roadmap rather than reading as a gap.
8. Photography★ We suggest: Keep MA
Modern AnimalAuthentic real-couple imagery, expanded to show the result of formation (the benefits of a joyful, lasting marriage), not only couples using the product.
New directionImagery mostly shows couples interacting with the product; authenticity is mixed, with some images reading stock or AI-generated.

Recommendation: set an authenticity standard and expand imagery to depict outcomes and benefits, with room to extend into visual storytelling (e.g. Instagram).

9. Texture & sacred-modern feel★ We suggest: Keep MA
Modern AnimalNatural texture + serif + subtle sacred echoes = premium, transcendent.
New directionClean and modern, but texture largely absent — reads closer to SaaS than sacred.
10. The pricing moment★ We suggest: Blend
Modern AnimalRestraint — show value simply; let the premium feel earn the price.
New directionValue-stacked block (“$531 total value → $225 today”).

Blend = keep the proof, lose the stack.

11. Headline typeface★ We suggest: Blend
Modern AnimalSacred-modern — a modern serif influence in the lead headlines for a premium, transcendent feel.
New directionA heavy sans display for headlines; serif used only as italic accent words.

The serif-for-emotion accent is already aligned; this is about the main headline face. Blend = introduce modern serif for hero and emotional headlines, keep sans for functional and interface text.

12. Interface & iconography cues★ We suggest: Blend
Modern AnimalSacred-modern restraint — lean on the rose motif and editorial spacing; few generic interface ornaments.
New directionThin-line icons, pill buttons, and rounded feature cards — clean, but reads like a tech product.

Blend = keep the clean, usable components, but reduce the generic software cues so the pages read sacred-premium rather than SaaS.
