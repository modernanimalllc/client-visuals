The revised schedule

The conference stays the fixed point. The Homepage and Clergy Hub go live and are verified the day before CFLA opens; the remaining six pages follow, each switching over as it is ready.

  1. Week of September 14

    Set up and prove the platform

    Design system established, and the routing approach tested on the live domain before anything is built on it.

  2. Week of September 21

    Homepage and Clergy Hub — design and copy

    Both pages run in parallel: wireframes, copy, and the Homepage design with motion. They go out for review together at the end of the week.

  3. Week of September 28

    Review, approval and build

    One approval round covering both pages, feedback integrated, then the component set and both page builds — alongside the staged migration and conference tracking.

  4. Week of October 5

    Final QA and fixes

    Design, content, accessibility, routing and tracking checked end to end. Everything is fixed and signed off by end of day October 6, so the 7th is cutover and verification only.

  5. Wednesday, October 7

    Go liveMilestone

    Homepage and Clergy Hub live and verified on themarriagegroup.com, with conference tracking and the /CFLA link in place.

  6. October 8 – 10

    CFLA, Charleston

    The Marriage Group's team is at the conference. Modern Animal's build continues through the event without pause.

  7. Weeks of October 12 and 19

    Remaining six pages

    Beginning October 8, the day after go-live. Copy and layout for all six, approved together in the week of October 12, then built on the design system established in the first wave — alongside the full checkout and order-level attribution work and search-visibility preservation.

  8. Friday, October 23

    Final deliveryMilestone

    All eight pages live. All scope complete.

  9. Weeks of October 26 and November 2

    Stabilization and handover

    Monitoring, fixes, documentation and team training.

Modern Animal will keep working to bring additional pages live ahead of the conference. Given where the delays have left the schedule, two pages is what can be committed to with confidence — anything beyond that will go live as soon as it is ready.
