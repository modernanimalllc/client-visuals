Revised scope of work

The Marriage Group — Modern Animal / Phase 1

The next two months represent a meaningful opportunity to build something that will make everything that follows faster, cleaner, and more effective. Rather than continuing execution against a strategy developed under different assumptions, this phase focuses on getting the fundamentals right — clarifying how TMG's brands relate to one another, sharpening the messaging, documenting how the marketing operation runs, and putting the right structure in place for an incoming hire. These deliverables aren't supporting materials — they're the foundation that TMG's team, new hire, and future partners will rely on to do their best work. Phase 2, when full campaign execution resumes, will be significantly stronger for it.

The monthly investment of $10,450 reflects the remaining balance due under the existing agreement, applied proportionately across both months. The scope below has been designed to deliver maximum value within that figure — replacing the original execution plan with a revised set of strategic and operational deliverables suited to where TMG is today.
